Careers by direction: roles, levels and how to start

A role here is a job title recorded against a course in Njia's catalogue. For each of the seven directions the questionnaire can point to, this page lists the roles that the most courses lead to, the levels those courses sit at, and how many are open to a learner with an E. It is a map of routes, not a forecast: Njia holds no employment rates or salaries per course, because Kenya does not publish them.

Counted from the catalogue on 13 November 2026. The 12 most common roles are shown for each direction. A count describes what Njia has listed, not what exists. See what the questionnaire can and cannot tell you and the pathways page for how the levels fit together.
