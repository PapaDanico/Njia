Counsellor kit: a class session and two handouts

A 50-minute class session for Form Four learners and school-leavers, with two pages to print and hand out. It uses what Njia already publishes: the questionnaire, the grade pages, the county pages and the fee and funding guidance. Nothing here needs an account, and learners' answers stay on their own phones.

The timings are a suggestion, not a standard. Counts are read from the catalogue on 13 November 2026. Njia lists 1,392 courses at 217 institutions in 47 counties; it does not list everything taught in Kenya, so a missing course is not proof that it does not exist.

Before the session

  • Learners need a phone with a browser, or a printed grade page from the table below. Njia works offline once opened.
  • Print the two handouts at the foot of this page: one worksheet and one list of questions per learner.
  • Tell learners the questionnaire is an exploration aid and not a validated test. It suggests directions to look into, it does not choose for them.

Cautions for the facilitator

  • No promises. A listed minimum grade is a first screen, not a placement offer. Programme requirements, subject grades and cut-offs change each cycle; confirm them with KUCCPS or the institution.
  • A fee not shown is not a free course. Where Njia shows no fee, the card says whether the institution publishes none or Njia could not verify one.
  • No outcomes data. Njia holds no employment rates or salaries per course, because Kenya publishes none. Do not present a job title as a promise.
  • Keep answers private. Ask learners not to compare questionnaire answers aloud unless they choose to. Njia stores nothing about them off their own phone; see privacy.

Handout A: my worksheet

Name (optional):

My KCSE mean grade

The direction the questionnaire pointed to

Three courses I want to look into (course, institution, level)

What each one asks for (entry grade and subjects)

What it costs, and who I could ask for help paying

My one next step, and who I will ask

Njia is a guide, not a placement service. Confirm everything on this sheet with KUCCPS or the institution before you apply.

Handout B: questions to ask an institution

  1. Which exact award is this, and who examines it: the institution, KNEC, NITA, CDACC or a university?
  2. Is the institution registered, and is this programme approved? Check the TVETA register for TVET colleges and the Commission for University Education for universities.
  3. What is the total cost for the whole course, and is the figure you quote per term, per semester or per year? How many of those are there?
  4. What is the entry mean grade, and which subject grades are required on top of it?
  5. In which months does the next intake start, and when does applying close?
  6. What else will I pay besides tuition: accommodation, meals, exams, attachment, uniform or tools?
  7. Do you offer work-study or help with funding, and what do I need to apply for it?

Write the answers down and keep the name of the person who gave them. Ring more than one place before you decide.
