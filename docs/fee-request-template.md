# Fee structure request (template)

Send to an institution's admissions or finance office. Fill in the square
brackets. One email per institution, naming its programmes as Njia lists them.

---

**Subject:** Request for your 2026/27 fee structure, for a free student guide

Dear Admissions Office,

Njia is a free, independent career and course guide for Kenyan school-leavers
(njiacareerpathways.work). We list [INSTITUTION]'s programmes, including
[PROGRAMME 1], [PROGRAMME 2] and [PROGRAMME 3], but we only show a fee when
the institution itself has published it.

Could you share your current fee structure for these programmes? The most
useful form is your official document (PDF or link), showing for each award:

1. tuition and any compulsory charges;
2. whether each figure is per semester, per trimester or per year;
3. how many of those periods the programme runs.

We do not change, estimate or average published figures. Each fee is shown
with its source and date, and corrected as soon as you send a newer one.

Thank you,
[NAME]
Njia
