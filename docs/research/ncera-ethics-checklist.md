# NCERA ethics and safeguarding checklist — pre-fieldwork

- [ ] Identify Kenyan research lead, sponsoring institution and independent scientific adviser
- [ ] Determine applicable institutional ethics review, NACOSTI requirements and school/site permissions
- [ ] Review Kenya Data Protection Act and ODPC guidance with qualified counsel
- [ ] Separate consumer PWA use from research recruitment and consent
- [ ] Obtain age-appropriate assent and parent/guardian permission for minors where required
- [ ] Define recruitment inclusion, exclusion and withdrawal without penalty
- [ ] Provide accessible non-digital alternative and accommodation process
- [ ] Specify data controller, processors, storage region, encryption, access and retention
- [ ] Keep contact identifiers separate from pseudonymised study responses
- [ ] Define breach reporting and safeguarding escalation contacts
- [ ] Prevent automated exclusion, stigma, deterministic career labels and unsupported fee claims
- [ ] Approve adverse-event monitoring and protocol-deviation log
- [ ] Register hypotheses and analysis plan before outcomes are examined
- [ ] Establish independent review of fairness and adverse subgroup effects
- [ ] Approve publication and deletion schedule before recruitment
