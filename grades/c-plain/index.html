About these figures

Entry grades are what the institution publishes, and published requirements sometimes conflict — where they do, Njia shows the lower bar and tells you to confirm, because a reader told to check keeps their options and a reader shown nothing does not. Fees are tuition only. Confirm both with the institution before you decide.
