What can I study with my KCSE grade?

TVET placement takes any grade, A to E, from anyone who sat KCSE from 2000 onward, and intake runs continuously. Pick your grade to see every course Njia lists that accepts it.

  • C plain — 378 courses
  • C- — 345 courses
  • D+ — 239 courses
  • D plain — 172 courses
  • D- — 123 courses
  • E — 115 courses

Grades above C+ are not listed separately because almost the whole catalogue is open to them — use Decide to filter by county, budget and interest instead.
