Privacy: what Njia keeps and what it counts

Njia is built so that your answers never leave your phone. This page says exactly what that means, what is the one exception, and the single thing Njia does count. It needs no account because there is no account.

What stays on your device

Everything you enter in Njia (questionnaire answers, plans, saved courses and objectives) is kept in your browser's local storage on this device. None of it is sent to a server or shared with anyone. There are no accounts and nothing to sign in to.

Njia does not use cookies, trackers or third-party analytics: nothing from outside the app is loaded or told anything about you.

The one thing you type that can leave

If you choose to submit the optional Feedback or Partner Inquiry form, whatever you type into that form is sent to us. It is the only data that leaves your device, and only if you submit it. Please leave out personal details you would not want read by a stranger.

The one thing Njia does count

When someone finishes the questionnaire, saves a course, starts an application or downloads a report, Njia asks its own server for a small file named after that step. All that gets recorded is that the step happened once more than yesterday. Nothing about you goes with it: no name, no device or session number, no answers, not even which course. Two steps from you look exactly like one step from two different people, so there is no trail back to anybody. It is the same kind of record any website keeps of a page being opened.

It exists because, without knowing how many young people reach the end, Njia cannot be funded or taken up by a school. If your browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control, Njia counts nothing. Offline, it counts nothing either. Like any website, Njia's host also keeps ordinary request counts for the pages it serves.

What you can do

  • Back up and move. The privacy panel in the app exports a backup file, or copies the backup as text if your browser blocks downloads (the WhatsApp, Facebook and Instagram browsers often do). Nothing syncs automatically between devices.
  • Wipe it. On a shared or borrowed phone, use Clear My Data before handing it back. It removes everything Njia stored on that device. It cannot recall a Feedback or Partner Inquiry you already sent.

What this page does not claim

Njia has not had a legal review under Kenya's Data Protection Act 2019. Because it holds no personal data on a server, little of the Act should apply, but that is our reading and not legal advice. If that changes, for example by adding accounts, this page changes first.
