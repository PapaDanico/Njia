# Twenty records close now; funding windows stay shut

About twenty fee figures, five licence readings, one ownership question and two new institutions can go into Njia's data today. No funding window that reaches a TVET or D/E-grade learner is open on 27 November 2026. On fees, the KUCCPS degree-programme list gives a Year 1 programme cost for **thirteen fee-less private-degree records whose award names match exactly**. Five of them were read twice or appear in two KUCCPS documents: Daystar Environmental Health, three Kabarak degrees and Baraton BEd (Science). The other eight were read once and fit the existing `once: true` convention in `KUCCPS_YEAR1_COST`. On licences, the TVETA register settles three of the seven open questions. Clive Irvine is licensed to 2029. Kenya Baptist Theological College is "Registered Only", which is not a licence. KQ Pride is classed **public** by TVETA. A fourth reading, KIM Nairobi, shows a licence that expired on 3 November 2026, and its date needs one check before it is written. NIBS, Moffat Bible College and Harmonics Air Centre were not found. Their cards should keep "Licence not checked by Njia" and should not say "unregistered". On course leads, Koitaleel Samoei University College and Sirisia TVC each publish award-level lists on their own domains. Mulango and Emining each publish one department's list. Kibabii TTI is still a Facebook page surrounded by three other Kibabii institutions. KMTC's per-campus question is unresolved, because its booklet says "Selected Campuses" for every pre-service programme. On funding, every dated 2026/27 window has closed, and no January 2027 window has been announced. Every figure in this report was **read through a WebSearch summary. No document was opened in full.** The researchers treated WebFetch as banned, although CLAUDE.md lifted that ban on 27 November. That matters most for KMTC, whose host answers a direct fetch.

## Thirteen KUCCPS Year 1 costs match fee-less private degrees by name

The column header reads "PROGRAMME COST" ([DEGREE_PROGRAMMES.pdf](https://statics.kuccps.net/uploads/globalFiles/DEGREE_PROGRAMMES.pdf)), and its programme pages label the same figure "YEAR 1 - Programme Cost" ([KUCCPS programme page](https://students-new.kuccps.net/programmes/detail/1175/)). A companion revision is dated **20 March 2024** ([RV2](https://statics.kuccps.net/uploads/globalFiles/DEGREE_20_03_2024_RV2.pdf)). The 2025/26 list came back with cut-offs and **no cost column** ([2025 list](https://statics.kuccps.net/uploads/globalFiles/DEGREE_PROGRAMMES_2025.pdf)). The costs are therefore about two and a half years old, and the list states neither an academic year nor whether statutory charges are included. KUCCPS is a national authority, so its figure meets the sourcing standard by itself. It still covers Year 1 only, so it stays a shown figure and never enters `total_fees_kes`, which is how the 64 existing entries are handled. ## The TVETA register settles three of seven licence questions

Every reading below comes from searches restricted to `tveta.go.ke`, whose summaries quote the accredited-institutions list ([TVETA register](https://www.tveta.go.ke/accredited-tvet-institutions/)). The register snapshot was reported as about 35 days old, so a renewal or a lapse since October may not show.

**Clive Irvine** is listed as "PCEA Chogoria Hospital Clive Irvine College of Nursing", **TVETA/PRIVATE/TVC/0101/2018, Registered and Licensed, expiry 2029-09-29**. The number came back without being put in the query. This settles the earlier reading of "no status shown", and the college also appears on the Nursing Council's approved list ([NCK](https://nckenya.com/approved-training-institutions/)). Write it. The register name ends "College of Nursing", not "of Health Sciences", but the hospital, county and number match.

**Kenya Baptist Theological College** is **TVETA/PRIVATE/TVC/0017/2026, "Registered Only", expiry 2027-01-07**. An aggregator gives the same number and the same date written day-first ([educationnewshub](https://educationnewshub.co.ke/kenya-baptist-theological-colleges-courses-requirements-location-how-to-join/)). No source defines "Registered Only". It does read as registered without a licence, so the card must not say "licensed". `regulatoryStatus()` needs a third state, or a note, that shows the register's own words. That carries more weight because of the July 2026 parliamentary order telling theology colleges to regularise.

**KQ Pride Training Centre** is **TVETA/PUBLIC/TVC/0015/2025, Registered and Licensed, expiry 2030-04-24**. Njia records the centre as private. It is Kenya Airways' training arm and an IATA-certified centre ([IATA](https://www.iata.org/en/services/certification/training-development/cbta-center/cbta-certified-companies/kenya-airways/437606/)). TVETA's public classification probably reflects the state's stake in Kenya Airways. The licence can be written. The **`fee_regime` should not change**, because a "public" category on the register does not put the centre on the consolidated Ksh 67,189 rate or the KUCCPS route. Changing it would trigger the national-figure mechanisms that `isVocationalCentre()` was written to stop. Record the register's category in the note.

**KIM Nairobi Branch** is **TVETA/PRIVATE/TVC/0205/2016, Registered and Licensed, expiry 2026-11-03**. Two queries that did not contain the number returned it, so by date the licence has expired. The register lists KIM branch by branch. Utawala, Eldoret and Narok are licensed to 2029 or 2030. Nakuru, Nyeri, Meru and Nyahururu have expired. None of the entries reflects the 20 April 2026 revocation ([The Standard](https://www.standardmedia.co.ke/health/amp/newsbeat/article/2001545821/tvet-authority-shuts-down-kenya-institute-of-management-over-lack-of-accreditation)) or the 22 April High Court stay ([The Star](https://www.the-star.co.ke/news/2026-04-22-court-halts-closure-of-kim-campuses)). The expiry date is **the same day CLAUDE.md records for Kenya Red Cross Training Institute's expired licence**, and that is the contamination pattern this project treats as a warning sign. Confirm the date on the KIM details page before writing "licence shown as expired". Keep `regulatory_dispute` in either case. The reported 9 July dismissal of TVETA's objection rests on KIM's own notice and one outlet ([Kenyans.co.ke](https://www.kenyans.co.ke/news/125129-high-court-rejects-tvetas-bid-halt-kim-operations)), so it is a report, not a ruling Njia can cite.

**NIBS, Moffat Bible College and Harmonics Air Centre stay unchecked.** For NIBS, a register-scoped search returned only the NYS Institute of Business Studies, a different public college. The one licence number found, for the Rongai campus (0038/2017), is aggregator-only ([teacher.co.ke](https://teacher.co.ke/?p=12934)). Moffat is spelled two ways and appears on neither the TVETA nor the CUE register. Its Wayland Baptist partnership ([Baptist Standard](https://baptiststandard.com/news/texas/wayland-baptist-university-partners-with-moffatt-bible-college-to-continue-work-in-kenya)) gives it no Kenyan standing. Harmonics was not in the parts of the KCAA approved-training-organisation lists the summaries covered ([KCAA 2025](https://kcaa.or.ke/sites/default/files/docs/pel/LIST-OF-APPROVED-TRAINING-ORGANIZATIONS-2025.pdf)). Its only public trace is a January 2026 linkage with Kenya Aeronautical College ([Capital FM](https://www.capitalfm.co.ke/business/2026/01/kenya-partners-with-china-university-to-train-aviation-professionals)), so it may train under that college's approval. In all three cases a search that found nothing proves nothing, so the cards should not say "unregistered".

## Koitaleel Samoei and Sirisia publish their own award lists; Kibabii TTI does not

**Koitaleel Samoei University College** (Mosoriot, Nandi, a constituent college of UoN) is missing from the register. Its 2026/27 joining instructions list degrees by school ([KSUC joining instructions](https://ksu.ac.ke/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/JOINING-INSTRUCTIONS-2026-.pdf)). Three programmes have more than that one document behind them. **BSc Agribusiness Management** has a KUCCPS page with code 3890187 ([prog 1197](https://students.kuccps.net/programmes/detail/1197/)). **Bachelor of Economics and Statistics** has code 3890199 and a C+ in Mathematics ([prog 1235](https://students.kuccps.net/programmes/detail/1235/)). **BEd (Arts)** has its own page, giving C+ aggregate with C in Mathematics and English or Kiswahili ([KSUC BEd Arts](https://ksu.ac.ke/index.php/education-arts/)). These three are safe to write at C+, with no fee because the SCFM applies, and with a null duration because KSUC publishes none. The other dozen programmes in the joining instructions (BEd ECE, BEd Science, BA Economics, BCom, the single-subject BScs and others) rest on one primary document from the institution's own domain, which also confirms the schools. That is enough under the widened listing standard. Two more things need deciding before KSUC goes in. Five records keep it off the thin-tail ratchet. Constituent colleges are not counted among CUE's 36 chartered universities, so whether it counts there needs a decision.

**Sirisia TVC** now has its own domain, which clears the earlier "one aggregator" blocker. Its Computing and Informatics page names **ICT Technician Level 5 (D+, two years) and Level 6 (C-, three years)** under CDACC, with January, May and September intakes ([Sirisia C&I](https://sirisiatvc.ac.ke/department-details/computing-informatics)). Its brochure gives Carpentry and Joinery Level 4 at "KCPE or KCSE D-", which is open entry under the either/or rule, and Accountancy Level 6 at C- ([brochure](http://sirisiatvc.ac.ke/storage/uploads/e_resource/1762950799-31139.pdf)). The summariser warned that the brochure's text extraction is jumbled. The ICT pair has the cleanest attribution and can be written from the college's own page. The brochure items need the page read directly. A county Sirisia VTC shows up in the same results ([Mabumbe](https://mabumbe.com/za/sirisia-vocational-training-center-vtc-bungoma-application-form-contacts-courses/)), so check the institution on every row.

**Mulango TVC** is public. KUCCPS lists its programmes at the consolidated Ksh 67,189 ([KUCCPS craft list, March 2024](https://statics.kuccps.net/uploads/globalFiles/CRAFT_18_03_2024_RV2.pdf)), and the report that it is private was not found. Its Business Studies page names four **Level 4** awards at one year, with entry "D(Minus) and an E": Accounting Technician, Secretarial Studies, HRM and Supply Chain ([Mulango Business](https://mulangotvc.ac.ke/departments/6)). KUCCPS corroborates Accounting Technician and Supply Chain only at **craft (Level 5)**, which is a different award, so the Level 4 rows still lack a second sighting. Mulango's own Level 5 and Level 6 tables ([Level 5](https://mulangotvc.ac.ke/programmes/level-5); [Level 6](https://mulangotvc.ac.ke/programmes/level-6)) together with the KUCCPS craft rows make **Craft in Accounting Technician and Craft Certificate in Supply Chain Management** the attributable additions. Two sources name them and give the Ksh 67,189 rate. The Electrical Engineering Technician question resolves against the "Level 4" reading, because Mulango's own Level 6 page lists that award as a diploma.

**Emining TTI** publishes per-level Business Department awards: five at Level 6 (C-) and four at Level 5 (D) ([Emining Business](https://eminingtti.ac.ke/business-department/)). Its homepage and admissions page contradict each other on Level 4 ("D- and above" against "D- & below") ([homepage](https://eminingtti.ac.ke/); [admissions](https://eminingtti.ac.ke/admissions-2/)). One page from the institution's own domain names each award and its level, which clears the old level conflict for business only. Without a second sighting the awards stay a strong lead. A KUCCPS search scoped to Emining is the next step, and it was not run.

**Kibabii TTI stays a lead.** Its only direct source is a Facebook page advertising KASNEB diplomas ([Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/people/Kibabii-Technical-Training-Institute/100094774608849/)), which is professional-body provision and not a TVET programme list. The same searches return Kibabii University's TVET directorate ([kibu.ac.ke](https://kibu.ac.ke/academic-programs/technical-and-vocational-education-and-training-tvet-programmes/)), Kibabii VTC and a Kibabii institute in Murang'a.

**KMTC** publishes an Academic Programmes Booklet dated 16 September 2025 ([booklet](http://kmtc.ac.ke/phocadownloadpap/academic-docs/KMTC-Academic-Programmes-Booklet-16-Sep-2025-.pdf)). It names campuses only for post-basic higher diplomas and gives "Selected Campuses" for pre-service programmes. Its own documents also disagree on Nutrition and Dietetics, giving both two years and three. The per-campus pre-service source is the intake advertisement ([September 2025 advert](https://kmtc.ac.ke/images/programmes/KMTC%20SEPTEMBER%202025%20PRE-SERVICE%20INTAKE%20ADVERTISEMENT.pdf)). Under the lifted ban that advert can be fetched, because `www.kmtc.ac.ke` answers directly. That is the cheapest route to closing several campus leads at once.

## Every dated window for TVET and low-grade learners has closed

The 2026/27 HEF windows for first-time and continuing students, university and TVET, were both **extended to 21 September 2026 and are closed** ([Citizen Digital](https://citizen.digital/article/ministry-of-education-orders-extension-of-helb-university-fund-applications-to-september-21-n389531); [KNA](https://www.kenyanews.go.ke/government-extends-application-deadline-for-tertiary-education-funding-to-september-21/)). That matches what f011 and f018 already say. **No January 2027 HEF window has been announced.** For NG-CDF, the 2025/26 constituency forms came out in November and December 2025 and closed between 9 and 16 January 2026 ([Nyatike, ngcdf.go.ke](https://ngcdf.go.ke/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/NYATIKE-BURSARY-APPLICATION-FORM-2025-2026.pdf); [West Mugirango](https://educationnews.co.ke/?p=492911)). No 2026/27 notice exists yet, and the "2027 CDF" calls in search results are Zambian. The December-to-January pattern is a strong expectation and not a published date. It does not belong on the Application Clock.

The county picture is the same. Kakamega's 2026/27 revolving fund ran 17 to 31 August through the HEF portal, a date that rests on one outlet ([Education News](https://educationnews.co.ke/?p=526250)). Makueni's VTC scholarship ran 9 to 16 July ([vtc.makueni.go.ke](https://vtc.makueni.go.ke/views/scholarship)). Bomet's VTC scholarship ran 20 January to 6 February. All three have closed. Nairobi, Nakuru, Uasin Gishu, Kisii, Machakos, Kiambu, Turkana and Kisumu have published no 2026/27 dates. Kericho's target of 27,100 sponsored TVET places ([KNA](https://www.kenyanews.go.ke/govt-targets-27100-kericho-youth-in-sponsorship-drive/)) is a County Commissioner's goal, not an application anyone can make, and its date is unclear. It stays a lead.

One fresh call is worth writing in as closed. **Equity Group Foundation's TVET Phase V form, uploaded November 2026, closed on 18 November 2026** ([Equity form](https://equitygroupfoundation.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/TVET-APPLICATION-FORM-Phase-V.pdf)). It is almost certainly the same scheme as f031, the KfW and Ministry of Education programme of 1,200 scholarships across 51 colleges ([Kenyans.co.ke](https://kenyans.co.ke/news/129295-germany-partners-ministry-offer-1200-scholarships-kenyans)). Add the closing date to f031 as a closed past window and leave the agriculture focus unconfirmed, because a second summary found nothing to support it. NITA's 2026 recognition-of-prior-learning drive was free and limited to **selected NYOTA beneficiaries**, and its results were issued in February 2026 ([Nairobi Leo](https://nairobileo.co.ke/news/article/25300/nita-issues-exercise-notice-to-nyota-beneficiaries)). The only route into it for a learner with no grade is therefore f030, NYOTA via *254#, which Njia already holds. The KCB item of 11 November ([Kenya Times](https://thekenyatimes.com/latest-kenya-times-news/kcb-opens-nationwide-scholarship-opportunities-for-select-kenyans-how-to-apply/)) is probably the secondary KCB Scholars cohort and not a TVET route.

## Conclusion

Fees have moved furthest. Ten private-degree records can carry a national authority's Year 1 cost today, and five more are one title check away. That is the largest single gain against the maintainer's top priority. The licence work found something the card logic does not yet handle. The register has at least three states (licensed, "Registered Only" and expired), a "public" category that does not match fee regime, and per-branch entries that ignore a court dispute. `regulatoryStatus()` should show the register's own words and not reduce them to licensed or not.

This pass also misread one rule. The researchers worked from the old "no WebFetch" instruction, which CLAUDE.md lifted on 27 November. For TVETA and KUCCPS this cost nothing, because both hosts still refuse direct connections. For `kmtc.ac.ke`, Sirisia's brochure, and the KIM details page whose expiry date needs checking, a direct read would settle what four search summaries could not. Those three fetches should come first in the next pass.
