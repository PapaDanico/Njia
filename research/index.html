02 / Fee provenance

What can we actually verify?

Published means institution-observed. Derived means calculated from a referenced rate. Illustrative and unsourced figures are not quotations. Missing fees do not mean free tuition.

Before you apply

Always confirm the current fee schedule, course availability and intake directly with the institution. Placement and financing are separate processes.

04 / Methodology

Read the fine print.
It matters.

All totals use linked course offerings as the denominator unless specified. A programme name may appear at several institutions. Null entry grade means open entry in Njia's catalogue semantics, not guaranteed admission.

No graduate employment, admission success or salary outcomes are inferred.
