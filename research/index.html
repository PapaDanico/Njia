Catalogue evidence: what is listed and how well it is sourced

A transparent view of what Njia has listed, how strong the fee evidence behind it is, and where coverage is thin. These statistics describe the Njia catalogue, not every institution or course in Kenya. They are not a national census or a guarantee of admission.

Counted from the catalogue on 13 November 2026. A course listing is one programme at one institution, so identical names at different institutions are separate listings. The same counts, row by row, are in the open data download.

Fee provenance

Every listing falls into exactly one group. A missing fee does not mean free tuition.

Entry grades

1,315 listings publish a minimum mean grade. 77 carry none, which Njia treats as open entry, not as guaranteed admission. A blank means the institution states no bar that Njia could attribute.

Read the fine print

All totals use course listings linked to a known institution as the denominator. No graduate employment, admission success or salary outcome is inferred, because Kenya publishes none per course.
