Primary-source research register

Kenyan education guidance changes by placement cycle. This register records the official sources and the precise limits of what Njia can infer. It is not a live admissions service, an accreditation certificate or an endorsement.

Research reviewed 13 November 2026. Confirm current programme details on the linked official websites.

KUCCPS placement eligibility and selection

Kenya Universities and Colleges Central Placement Service · Reviewed 2026-11-13

KUCCPS lists baseline placement grades: degree C+ and above; diploma C/C-; certificate D+/D; artisan D-/E. Actual programme-specific requirements, priorities and capacities also govern selection.

How Njia should use this: Show these as general entry guidance, not guaranteed eligibility for a named course.

Accredited TVET institutions and programme verification

State Department for TVET · Reviewed 2026-11-13

Before selecting a TVET provider, verify registered-and-licensed status, licence expiry and approval to offer the specific programme using the TVETA register.

How Njia should use this: Require institution and programme-level evidence; never infer approval from a college name alone.

Student-centred higher education funding

Universities Fund · Reviewed 2026-11-13

Placement and financing are separate processes. Public university scholarships depend on eligibility and assessed need; students must apply for funding.

How Njia should use this: Never treat a KUCCPS placement as an automatic scholarship or promise an award.
