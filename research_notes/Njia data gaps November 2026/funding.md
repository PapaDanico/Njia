# Funding for Kenyan TVET learners and low-grade school-leavers, as of 27 November 2026

How this was read: every finding below comes from WebSearch result summaries (16 searches). **No document was opened in full.** WebFetch was not used, including on hef.co.ke: the project's CLAUDE.md forbids WebFetch outright ("WebFetch is off"), and that standing rule outranks the brief. "Official" below means the result URL is on the body's own domain, read through a search summary.

Headline: **I found no funding window open on 27 November 2026 that reaches a D, D- or E learner and has a published closing date.** Every dated 2026 window I found has closed. The NG-CDF 2026/27 cycle has not been announced, and in past cycles constituencies opened in December and closed in January.

## HEF and HELB: open or announced windows for January 2027 or 2026/27

### Takeaway
Both 2026/27 windows (first-time and continuing, university and TVET) are closed. Each was extended to 21 September 2026. No January 2027 window was announced in any result.

### Cited Findings
- First-time 2026/27 applications for university and TVET students opened on 10 July 2026 through the HEF portal (hef.co.ke) — [The Star](https://www.the-star.co.ke/news/2026-07-10-helb-opens-202627-loan-scholarship-applications); [Capital FM](https://capitalfm.africa/helb-opens-2026-2027-first-time-loan-scholarship-applications-for-university-and-tvet-students/); [People Daily](https://peopledaily.digital/news/helb-opens-2026-27-first-time-loan-and-scholarship-applications-for-university-tvet-students)
- The Ministry of Education extended the first-time university and TVET deadline from 31 August to **21 September 2026**. That date is past, so the window is CLOSED — [Citizen Digital](https://citizen.digital/article/ministry-of-education-orders-extension-of-helb-university-fund-applications-to-september-21-n389531); [Kenyans.co.ke](https://www.kenyans.co.ke/news/126768-education-ministry-extends-university-tvet-funding-application-deadline-21-september); [KNA (kenyanews.go.ke, government)](https://www.kenyanews.go.ke/government-extends-application-deadline-for-tertiary-education-funding-to-september-21/)
- Continuing students' subsequent-loan deadline was extended from 31 August to **21 September 2026**. CLOSED. Applications went through the HELB app or *642# — [Kenyans.co.ke](https://www.kenyans.co.ke/news/126851-helb-extends-loan-application-deadline-continuing-students); [Education News](https://educationnews.co.ke/?p=527879); [Nation](https://nation.africa/kenya/news/education/state-extends-helb-applications-deadline--5582500)
- Sources disagree on when the continuing-student window opened: 1 June in one report, 8 July in another. A K24 article gives a closing date of 8 September, but it predates the extension — summary of [Kenyans.co.ke](https://www.kenyans.co.ke/news/126851-helb-extends-loan-application-deadline-continuing-students) and [K24](https://k24.digital/lifestyle/money/15-things-students-need-to-know-about-helb-2026-27-loan-scholarship-applications)
- By the first deadline, 789,422 funding applications had been received, and Ksh 22.12 billion had been disbursed to continuing university students and TVET trainees — [Kenyans.co.ke](https://www.kenyans.co.ke/news/126768-education-ministry-extends-university-tvet-funding-application-deadline-21-september) (one outlet in the summary; treat as single-sourced)
- The Standard reports the government "reopens university funding window as thousands miss deadline". That is the September extension, not a new window — [The Standard](https://www.standardmedia.co.ke/education/article/2001556887/govt-reopens-university-funding-window-as-thousands-miss-deadline)
- HELB says an admission letter is required before applying, and that the application is free and only made through the official HEF and HELB platforms — [Education News](https://educationnews.co.ke/?p=523481); [People Daily](https://peopledaily.digital/news/helb-opens-2026-27-first-time-loan-and-scholarship-applications-for-university-tvet-students)

This matches the scholarship-stream figures already in CLAUDE.md, and must not be set against the HELB loan figures.

### Inferences
- A January 2027 TVET intake will likely need its own HEF window, as the May 2026 TVET intake presumably had, but no result announces one. Nothing should go on the Application Clock.
- This confirms the existing f011/f018 note that the window closed on 21 September after an extension.

### Gaps
- No January 2027 or May 2027 HEF/HELB window was found. hef.co.ke was not opened (WebFetch is forbidden), so a notice posted only on the portal would not show up here.
- No TVET-specific closing date separate from the university one was found.

## NG-CDF bursaries 2026/27

### Takeaway
No 2026/27 national or constituency window is published yet. In the 2025/26 cycle constituencies issued forms in late November or December and closed in early to mid January 2026.

### Cited Findings

- The "2027 CDF" results that come up in searches are **Zambian** (fundsforngos.org) and do not apply to Kenya — [fundsforNGOs](https://www2.fundsforngos.org/individuals/cfps-2027-constituency-development-fund-zambia/)

### Inferences
- The 2026/27 constituency forms are likely to appear in December 2026 with closing dates in January 2027. That is a pattern, not a published date, and it must not be recorded. CLAUDE.md already records a five-day 2025/26 window at one constituency (5 to 9 January).

### Gaps
- No 2026/27 constituency notice with dates was found. The national ngcdf.go.ke news page surfaced but showed no dated notice in the summary.

No 2026/27 window dates were found — [nakuru.go.ke](https://www.nakuru.go.ke/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/24TH-APRIL-2026-BURSARY.pdf); [Capital FM](https://capitalfm.africa/nakuru-county-prioritises-learners-with-special-needs-in-2026-bursary-allocation/)

- **Bomet:** the 2026 full scholarship for newly admitted trainees at its 33 VTCs ran from 20 January to 6 February 2026. CLOSED — [Education News](https://educationnews.co.ke/?p=497172); [bomet.go.ke](https://bomet.go.ke/call-for-bursary-allocation). Already in Njia's list.

### Gaps
- No 2026/27 window with dates was found for Nairobi, Nakuru, Uasin Gishu, Kisii, Machakos, Makueni, Kiambu, Turkana or Kisumu.

## NITA and Ministry of Education: 2026/27 programmes for out-of-school youth and recognition of prior learning

### Takeaway
NITA's 2026 recognition-of-prior-learning drive was **free and tied to NYOTA beneficiaries**. Its screening and results ran in January and February 2026. No 2026/27 round open to the general public was found.

### Cited Findings
- Recognition-of-prior-learning certification for over 40,000 youth, run by NITA under the NYOTA project (World Bank-supported). Reported allocation Ksh 229m, screening free of charge, and officials warned against paying anyone — [The Standard](https://www.standardmedia.co.ke/education/article/2001529885/kenya-rolls-out-rpl-certification-for-40000-youth-to-boost-employability); [Education News](https://educationnews.co.ke/?p=486703); [KNA](https://www.kenyanews.go.ke/173392-2)
- A January 2026 notice said the exercise was **for successfully selected NYOTA beneficiaries** with prior skills. Results were due 18 February and transcripts from 23 February 2026, so it is PAST — [Nairobi Leo](https://nairobileo.co.ke/news/article/25300/nita-issues-exercise-notice-to-nyota-beneficiaries); [KNA](https://www.kenyanews.go.ke/173392-2)
- Officials said certification would continue beyond the World Bank programme, because recognition of prior learning is part of NITA's core mandate. That is a statement of intent, not a scheduled round — [The Standard](https://www.standardmedia.co.ke/education/article/2001529885/kenya-rolls-out-rpl-certification-for-40000-youth-to-boost-employability)
- The Ministry of Education's State Department for TVET programme-based budget for FY 2026/27 to 2028/29 exists on education.go.ke. It was not read beyond its title — [education.go.ke](https://education.go.ke/sites/default/files/2025-10/SD-TVET%20PBB%20FY%202026-27%20to%202028-29%20Submitted%20to%20TNT%20by%208th%20Oct_0.pdf)
- July 2026 KNA items on NYOTA-related NITA activity surfaced without readable detail — [KNA](https://www.kenyanews.go.ke/?p=175466)

### Inferences
- Recognition of prior learning reaches people with no KCSE grade at all. For now its only open route is being a NYOTA beneficiary, which Njia already records as f030 (applied for via *254#). That fits the "lead" Njia already has on whether the RPL strand has a public route.

### Gaps
- No 2026/27 recognition-of-prior-learning round open to the general public, and no Ministry of Education programme for out-of-school youth with an application window, was found.
- The State Department for TVET budget document was not read, so any programme targets inside it are unknown.
