# Regulatory registration status of seven Kenyan private colleges (read 27 November 2026)

Method note: the project's CLAUDE.md bans WebFetch, and that maintainer instruction outranks the brief's "try WebFetch first", so no host was fetched or re-tested. Every reading below comes from a WebSearch summary. Readings marked "TVETA register" come from searches restricted to `tveta.go.ke`, whose summaries quote the register's institution entries (accredited-institutions list and institution-details pages). No registration number being checked was put into a query. The register snapshot was reported as about 35 days old, so a status read today may lag a renewal or a lapse.

## KIM (Kenya Institute of Management), Nairobi: TVETA entry, and the April 2026 revocation

### Takeaway
On the TVETA register, "Kenya Institute of Management – Nairobi Branch" is TVETA/PRIVATE/TVC/0205/2016, Registered and Licensed, with an **expiry of 2026-11-03**, which has already passed. Two separate queries returned this, neither containing the number. The register still lists KIM branches individually, with no sign of the 20 April 2026 revocation. The High Court stay of 22 April 2026 is widely reported. Its reported follow-up, the 9 July dismissal of TVETA's objection, rests on KIM's own notice plus one outlet.

### Cited Findings
- TVETA register: KIM Nairobi Branch (S/N 1752), private TVC, Nairobi County, TVETA/PRIVATE/TVC/0205/2016, "Registered and Licensed", expiry 2026-11-03 — [TVETA Accredited TVET Institutions](https://www.tveta.go.ke/accredited-tvet-institutions/)
- Same register, other KIM entries: Utawala Campus TVETA/PRIVATE/TVC/0131/2018, licensed, expiry 2029-07-02; Eldoret TVETA/PRIVATE/TVC/0257/2016, licensed, expiry 2029-11-26; Narok TVETA/PRIVATE/TVC/0014/2017, licensed, expiry 2030-11-05; Nakuru Campus, expired licence (expiry 2026-09-26); Nyeri, expired (2025-11-01); Meru and Nyahururu, expired (2023 and 2022) — [TVETA Accredited TVET Institutions](https://www.tveta.go.ke/accredited-tvet-institutions/)
- The brief's Kitengela number (TVETA/PRIVATE/TVC/0124/2018) did not come back in any register reading this pass. No main or head-office KIM entry appeared separately from "Nairobi Branch".
- Aggregator only, not the register: Mombasa TVETA/PRIVATE/TVC/0016/2017, Nyeri 0207/2016, Thika 0036/2019, Nanyuki 0011/2018 — [teacher.co.ke](https://teacher.co.ke/?p=10643)
- TVETA revoked KIM's accreditation and ordered all its campuses closed — [The Standard](https://www.standardmedia.co.ke/health/amp/newsbeat/article/2001545821/tvet-authority-shuts-down-kenya-institute-of-management-over-lack-of-accreditation); [People Daily](https://peopledaily.digital/news/tveta-revokes-accreditation-of-kenya-institute-of-management-orders-immediate-closure/amp)
- On 22 April 2026, Justice Musyoka (Milimani High Court) granted leave for judicial review, with the leave operating as a stay of the revocation and closure — [The Star](https://www.the-star.co.ke/news/2026-04-22-court-halts-closure-of-kim-campuses); [The Standard](https://www.standardmedia.co.ke/article/2001546045/court-suspends-tveta-order-to-close-kim); [Nation](https://nation.africa/kenya/news/kim-wins-first-round-in-tvet-closure-row--5525000); [Citizen Digital](https://citizen.digital/article/kim-campuses-closure-directive-suspended-as-govt-upholds-certificates-validity-n381319)
- KIM resumed operations after the stay — [Capital FM](https://www.capitalfm.co.ke/news/2026/04/kim-resumes-operations-after-court-halts-tveta-enforcement/); [The Star, 23 April 2026](https://www.the-star.co.ke/news/2026-04-23-kims-resumption-after-court-reprieve-draws-mixed-reactions)
- The High Court reportedly dismissed TVETA's preliminary objection (on alternative dispute resolution) on 9 July 2026. That rests on KIM's notice and one outlet — [The Kenya Times](https://thekenyatimes.com/latest-kenya-times-news/relief-for-kim-students-as-court-delivers-verdict-on-college-accreditation/); [Kenyans.co.ke](https://www.kenyans.co.ke/news/125129-high-court-rejects-tvetas-bid-halt-kim-operations)

### Inferences
- The register entries are per branch and show ordinary licence states. Nothing in the readings reflects either the revocation or the stay, so the register is not a current account of the dispute. The cards should keep `regulatory_dispute` and name both events.
- On 27 November the Nairobi Branch licence reads as expired by date. The register may also not yet show a renewal.
- Contamination check: the 2026-11-03 expiry is the same date the project's CLAUDE.md records for Kenya Red Cross Training Institute's expired licence. Two unseeded queries returned it for KIM, so it is probably genuine. A same-date coincidence is still the shape this project treats as suspicious, so confirm it on the details page before writing.

### Gaps
- No final judgment on the merits of KIM's judicial review was found.
- No register reading for a KIM "main campus" distinct from the Nairobi Branch, and none for Kitengela.

### Gaps

- Watch the name collision with NYS Institute of Business Studies.

### Gaps

- Context: the parliamentary 90-day regularisation order on theology colleges (July 2026, recorded in CLAUDE.md) may bear on Moffat. Its current status is unknown.

## KQ Pride Centre (Kenya Airways): public or private

### Takeaway
TVETA register: "KQ Pride Training Centre", **public** TVC, Nairobi, TVETA/PUBLIC/TVC/0015/2025, Registered and Licensed, expiry 2030-04-24. This came back on an unseeded query. TVETA classes it as PUBLIC, while the operator is Kenya Airways, a listed company in which the government holds a stake.

### Cited Findings
- TVETA register: KQ Pride Training Centre, TVETA/PUBLIC/TVC/0015/2025, Nairobi, Registered and Licensed, expiry 2030-04-24 — [TVETA Accredited TVET Institutions](https://www.tveta.go.ke/accredited-tvet-institutions/)
- It is the training arm of Kenya Airways and an IATA-certified (CBTA) centre and Regional Training Partner — [IATA, KQ Pride Training Centre](https://www.iata.org/en/services/certification/training-development/cbta-center/cbta-certified-companies/kenya-airways/437606/)
- Kenya Airways opened the centre to the public and other operators — [The Standard](https://www.standardmedia.co.ke/article/2000000927/kenya-airways-training-centre-goes-public)

### Inferences
- "PUBLIC" in TVETA's number is TVETA's category, probably reflecting the state's ownership interest in Kenya Airways. It does not mean the centre is on the consolidated public-TVET fee or the KUCCPS placement route. Njia should not change KQ's `fee_regime` on this alone.

### Gaps
- No TVETA explanation of why a Kenya Airways unit is classed as public.
- The register name ("KQ Pride Training Centre") matches the institution by name, not by an explicit Kenya Airways link in the entry.
